# Velvet Gremlins — Visual Style Guide

> Version 1.0 — Phase 1 (Founder Edition)
> Last updated: 2026-09-06

---

## 1. Brand Identity

**Velvet Gremlins** is a premium digital collectible project featuring unique mischievous fantasy creatures. The visual identity sits at the intersection of **dark fantasy**, **cosmic celestial wonder**, and **designer collectible luxury** — creatures that are simultaneously mysterious, adorable, and undeniably premium.

### Core Aesthetic Pillars

| Pillar | Description |
|---|---|
| **Dark Fantasy** | Rich, shadowy atmosphere with arcane and royal undertones |
| **Cosmic / Celestial** | Galaxy textures, starfields, nebula colors, floating astral elements |
| **Premium Collectible** | Designer-toy quality, high detail, luxurious materials |
| **Soft & Plush** | Velvet/fur body texture — tactile, cuddly, yet otherworldly |
| **Mysterious but Cute** | Mischievous expressions, tiny fangs, endearing postures |

### Overall Mood

- Enchanting, luxurious, otherworldly
- A sense of discovering a rare artifact from a cosmic realm
- Playful mischief balanced with regal elegance
- Premium unboxing feeling — each Gremlin should feel like a treasure

### Primary Colors

| Swatch | Color | Hex | Usage |
|---|---|---|---|

| 🟪 | **Cosmic Purple** | `#2D1B4E` | Deep purple tones, cloak, body shadows |
| 💜 | **Nebula Violet** | `#6B3FA0` | Mid-tone purples, ear interiors, accents |
| 🟣 | **Astral Amethyst** | `#9B59D0` | Highlights, gemstones, magical effects |
| ✨ | **Royal Gold** | `#D4A843` | Crowns, jewelry, filigree, metallic accents |
| 🌟 | **Celestial Gold** | `#F0C75E` | Gold highlights, light-catching edges |
| ⚪ | **Ivory Fur** | `#F5F0E8` | Chest/belly fur trim, soft contrast |
| 🔮 | **Galaxy Eye Violet** | `#7B4FBF` | Eye iris base, mystical glow |
| ⭐ | **Starlight White** | `#FFFFFF` | Eye reflections, star sparkles |

### Secondary / Accent Colors

| Color | Hex | Usage |
|---|---|---|
| **Deep Plum** | `#1A0F2E` | Background atmospherics |
| **Warm Charcoal** | `#1C1C24` | Subtle body texture variation |
| **Soft Lavender** | `#C8A2E8` | Gentle glow effects, inner ear highlight |
| **Rose Gold** | `#C08850` | Warm metal accent variation |

### Color Rules

- **Never** use flat, saturated primary colors (pure red, blue, green)
- All purples should feel **deep and rich**, never neon
- Gold should look **warm and metallic**, not yellow
- Maintain strong contrast between dark body and gold/purple accents
- Backgrounds should be deep and atmospheric, never stark white (unless character is presented as a sticker/cutout)

## 3. Materials & Textures

### Body Fur / Velvet

- **Appearance**: Dense, short-pile velvet or plush fur
- **Quality**: Soft, light-absorbing with subtle sheen at edges
- **Color**: Near-black with deep purple undertones
- **Detail**: Individual fur strands visible at close range; galaxy-like sparkle embedded in fur
- **Light behavior**: Absorbs light in shadows, catches highlights at silhouette edges

### Gold Metal (Crown, Jewelry, Filigree)

- **Appearance**: Polished, warm gold with slight antique depth
- **Quality**: Premium — ornate engravings, celestial motifs (stars, moons, suns)
- **Reflections**: Strong specular highlights, soft warm reflections
- **Motifs**: Star-points, crescent moons, compass roses, orbital rings

### Gemstones (Crown, Ear Jewelry)

- **Type**: Amethyst / purple crystals
- **Appearance**: Faceted, translucent, internally lit with a gentle glow
- **Placement**: Crown centerpiece, ear dangles, floating halo crystal
- **Light behavior**: Internal refraction, soft purple luminescence

### Cloak / Fabric

- **Material**: Heavy velvet or dark silk
- **Color**: Black-purple with gold celestial embroidery
- **Edge detail**: Gold filigree trim, star patterns woven in
- **Weight**: Should look heavy and regal, draping naturally

### Ivory/White Fur Trim

- **Placement**: Chest ruff, cloak lining edges
- **Quality**: Soft, fluffy, warm contrast against dark body
- **Color**: Warm ivory, not stark white

## 4. Lighting

### Primary Light Source

- **Angle**: Front-top, slightly left of center (~45° above, ~15° left)
- **Quality**: Soft, diffused — no harsh shadows
- **Color temperature**: Cool-neutral with slight warm bias on gold elements

### Ambient / Fill Light

- Subtle purple/violet ambient fill from all directions
- Creates a "glowing from within" feeling
- Ear interiors and eye regions slightly self-luminous

### Rim / Edge Light

- Faint celestial rim light along body silhouette
- Enhances the plush texture and separates from background
- Slightly purple-tinted

### Magical Glow Effects

- Floating crystals emit a soft purple glow
- Eyes have internal galaxy-like luminescence
- Gemstones have subtle inner light
- Gold jewelry catches and reflects ambient light warmly

## 5. Character Proportions

### Body Structure

| Feature | Proportion |
|---|---|
| **Head-to-body ratio** | ~1:1.2 (large head, compact body) |
| **Eye-to-face ratio** | ~40-45% of face width (oversized, expressive) |
| **Ear-to-head ratio** | Each ear ~80-100% of head height (large, prominent) |
| **Body shape** | Compact, rounded, slightly bottom-heavy (plush toy proportions) |
| **Limbs** | Short, stubby, with visible paw pads |
| **Tail** | Long, fluffy, curled upward with decorative charm at tip |

### Pose Guidelines

- Default pose: Seated, front-facing, slightly tilted head
- Paws visible, displaying purple paw pads
- Tail curling to one side
- Ears alert and spread
- Expression: Mischievous confidence (slight smirk, visible tiny fangs)

## 6. Eye Style

### Structure

- **Shape**: Large, round, slightly asymmetric (one slightly larger for character)
- **Iris**: Deep galaxy/nebula pattern — violet, purple, with distant stars and swirling cosmic dust
- **Pupil**: Dark center blending into galaxy pattern (no hard black pupil edge)
- **Sclera**: Visible, off-white with subtle warm tone
- **Catchlights**: Multiple small star-like reflections, not standard circular highlights

### Characteristics

- Eyes should feel like windows into a galaxy/universe
- Depth — the iris should look like you can "fall into it"
- Internal luminescence — eyes glow softly
- Each Gremlin's galaxy pattern should be unique
- Expression is carried primarily through eye size, angle, and brow position

## 7. Crown & Jewelry Style

### Crown

- **Material**: Ornate gold with purple amethyst gemstones
- **Design**: Multi-pointed with curved horn-like tines
- **Details**: Celestial engravings, embedded gemstones
- **Placement**: Sits between/behind ears, integrated with horns
- **Character horns**: Natural gold-colored curved horns emerge naturally, crown complements them

### Ear Jewelry

- **Type**: Gold chains, dangling charms
- **Motifs**: Stars, moons, geometric celestial shapes, small gemstones
- **Placement**: Piercings and clips along ear edges
- **Movement**: Should suggest gentle swaying/motion

### Neck / Chest Jewelry

- **Primary piece**: Gold compass rose or celestial pendant
- **Chain**: Ornate gold, with smaller decorative elements
- **Style**: Regal, elaborate, layered

### Body Accessories

- **Gold bands/cuffs**: On arms/wrists with celestial engravings
- **Filigree**: Gold decorative patterns on cloak
- **Tail charm**: Gold ring or orbital charm at tail tip with dangling star/gem elements

### Floating Halo / Crystal

- **Element**: Floating purple crystal or astral halo above head
- **Position**: Slightly above and behind crown
- **Behavior**: Suggests levitation, magical energy
- **Glow**: Soft purple luminescence

## 8. Clothing Style

### Primary Garment: Royal Cloak

- Dark purple-black velvet
- Gold celestial embroidery (stars, constellations)
- Gold filigree trim along edges
- Ivory/white fur lining visible at collar and edges
- Draped regally, slightly open at front to show chest pendant

### Future Gremlin Clothing Variations

- Each Gremlin may have a distinct outfit, but must maintain:
  - Premium material feel (velvet, silk, brocade)
  - Gold accent integration
  - Celestial/cosmic motifs
  - Purple color family presence
  - Consistent accessory quality level

## 10. Rules for Future Gremlins

### Consistency Requirements

1. **Body type**: Always a Gremlin — compact, plush, big ears, big eyes, small body
2. **Eye style**: Always galaxy/cosmic eyes (color may vary within purple-violet-blue range)
3. **Material quality**: Always premium — velvet/plush body, gold-quality metals, gemstones
4. **Mood**: Always mischievous-cute-premium (never scary, never purely comical)
5. **Color family**: Primary palette must always include black/dark purple + gold + a signature accent
6. **Detail level**: Must always be "collectible card" / "designer toy" quality — highly detailed
7. **Silhouette**: Must be strong and recognizable — a Velvet Gremlin should be identifiable in silhouette

### Variation Opportunities

- **Body color**: Can shift within dark spectrum (deep blue, deep teal, etc.) — but purple must remain present
- **Accent color**: Signature accent may vary (emerald, ruby, sapphire) alongside mandatory purple/gold
- **Accessories**: Unique jewelry sets, different crowns/headwear, varied charms
- **Clothing**: Different garments while maintaining premium material quality
- **Expression**: Range of mischievous emotions
- **Rarity elements**: Special editions may add unique effects (particle auras, animated elements, holographic textures)

### Absolute Do-Nots

- ❌ No flat/matte coloring — always rich, textured, dimensional
- ❌ No simplified/chibi/low-detail versions for primary artwork
- ❌ No neon or fluorescent colors
- ❌ No modern/tech aesthetics — always fantasy/celestial
- ❌ No background clutter competing with the character
- ❌ No generic/stock art feeling — every piece must feel hand-crafted
- ❌ No changes to the Founder (#000) design — it is locked as the canonical reference

## 11. File Naming Convention

```
velvet-gremlin-[NUMBER]-[NAME]-[VARIANT].{ext}
```

**Examples:**
- `velvet-gremlin-000-founder-original.jpg`
- `velvet-gremlin-000-founder-clean.jpg`
- `velvet-gremlin-001-[name]-artwork.png`

### Numbering

- `#000` — Founder (reserved, 1/1)
- `#001` — `#999` — Standard collection
- Numbers are always zero-padded to 3 digits
