# 🐾 Velvet Gremlins

> Premium digital collectibles — mischievous cosmic creatures from the dark celestial realm.

---

## About

**Velvet Gremlins** is a premium digital collectible / NFT project built around unique, mischievous fantasy creatures. Each Gremlin is a hand-crafted character with a distinct personality, adorned in celestial gold and cosmic amethyst, wrapped in dark velvet and starlight.

The project combines **dark fantasy**, **cosmic wonder**, and **designer collectible luxury** into a cohesive visual universe.

### Visual Identity

- 🖤 Dark fantasy aesthetic with cosmic/celestial themes
- ✨ Premium designer collectible quality
- 🧸 Soft velvet/plush creature appearance
- 👁️ Expressive galaxy-like eyes
- 👑 Ornate gold crowns and celestial jewelry
- 🌌 Deep black, purple, violet, and gold color palette

---

## The Founder

**Velvet Gremlin #000 — Founder** is the genesis character of the collection.

- **Edition:** 1/1 (unique)
- **Role:** Founder / Origin character
- **Status:** Artwork preserved, pre-mint

The Founder establishes the canonical visual identity that all future Velvet Gremlins will follow. It is a locked design and will not be modified.
